What Green Gage’s Is

Green Gage’s is an interdisciplinary research and educational platform exploring how matter becomes mind, how experience becomes meaning, and how science, philosophy, and symbolic interpretation can speak to one another without being collapsed into each other.

The Conversation Format

Episodes are thoughtful, unscripted conversations guided by curiosity rather than rigid interviews. The host prepares carefully, but guests do not need to memorize answers.

What Guests Can Expect
