Cannabis Aroma Intelligence

The conventional indica / sativa / hybrid system is a botanical classification being used as a consumer-experience guide. It does not reliably predict flavour, effect, or purchase behaviour.

There is a better map.

The framework

Cannabis Aroma Intelligence Framework

Terpene profiles provide a more meaningful basis for differentiating a cannabis portfolio. The framework maps a company's strain portfolio across three publicly revealed aroma clusters:

  • Fruity
  • Dessert
  • Earthy

Case study

A more useful portfolio question

A leading Canadian licensed producer used the framework to shift the portfolio question from “Do we have enough indica?” toward “Which aroma clusters are undersupplied in our range?”

The resulting intelligence informed strain selection and product development decisions.
